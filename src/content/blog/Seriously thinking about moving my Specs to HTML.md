I finished reading the twit/article I read on [[The right format for AI Specs]]. It kept me wondering about it again. Specially because inside the article there is a reference to another article of the same author called [Making Playgrounds using Claude Code](https://x.com/trq212/article/2017024445244924382).

The combination of these two made think why I haven't used this approach in the past. Why was I so blind in love with markdown outputs, when this way is more expressive when you are still figuring out what to do.

I tested it quickly on my `Chief of Staff` agent that connects to Azure DevOps and Todoist to brainstorm ways of caching the work breakdown structure and I liked it. Then I tested it again to brainstorm the format of the `tasks.json` structure it is recommending me; I liked it too. I could even ask it to create me a dashboard of all the tasks from the different projects in another playground. It's ridiculously simple and visual.

As I write this, I'm getting more and more ideas. I need more time and more time.
