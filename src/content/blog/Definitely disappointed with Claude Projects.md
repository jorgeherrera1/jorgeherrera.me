So I really gave it a try. Not a huge try because I was honestly [[Disappointed with Claude Projects|disappointed from the very beginning]]. But I did try it. The experience is terrible, to be honest.

I was expecting Claude Projects to be superior to [[Cursor Projects nailed it|Cursor Projects]]. Maybe the wait time after they announced it made me believe that they really nailed it and they were just fine-tuning it for general usage.

It's a very poor DX, IMHO. There is the Agent Coordinator that does not coordinate anything. It just opens threads and passes over my same message. I was expecting a bit of prompt engineering fine tuning my bare request. I have to baby sit the _freaking_ threads.

I am working on a knowledge graph that has a visual representation and I tried to implement a new feature that extends existing graph nodes. I have to keep my eyes on the threads and I barely moved the needle trying to implement the feature. I'm guilty of not sitting for 2-3 hours to get it done. I stepped away several times a day, but every time I came back, my input was expected. I needed to steer individual agents. As I said, the coordinator agent just copied/pasted my bad-written prompt. Yeah, I know models are good at figuring it out, but come on.

I did not know if I was on a branch, on which branch, or in a worktree either. What's the point of a coordinator if I cannot do parallel work because I don't know if I'm going to be messing stuff up.

Orca set the bar high, I know. Or maybe I just liked that DX in particular. But I feel Claude let me down here. It was a bad moment for letting me down because I was already considering downgrading my Claude Max plan. I just did it. I did not do it because of Claude Projects. I did it because SpaceX AI unified the subscription of X + Grok + Cursor and I found it particularly interesting to try out. So yeah, the disappointment kind of pushed me to try different things. Cursor Projects is way better. Even [[Grok Bot is cool|Grok Bot]] spinning Cursor Agents is better.
