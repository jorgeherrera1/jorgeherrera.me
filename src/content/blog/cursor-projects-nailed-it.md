I have been trying Cursor projects for the last two days and I feel they nailed it. Context seems to be preserved well between interactions. The agents that the orchestrator spins off are delivering good results (most of the times), and so far it feels like a solid workflow.

Grok 4.6 is solid as well. This is the very first time I am thinking on using something that is not Claude. Claude has been amazing so far. No complains. Maybe it’s because I need to look outside the window more often but I never felt they have much competition, as an ecosystem. Claude Design, Claude Desktop, Claude Code, the add-ins for Microsoft 365, they pretty much cover the entire knowledge work landscape. 

Now with Grok Bot, I feel fresh air. Real innovation. As I said Grok 4.6 feels solid. Feels like Opus 5. Maybe not Fable but still a very good model.
